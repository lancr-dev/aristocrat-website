ABOUT US

OUR LEGACY AND HISTORY

The story of The Aristocrat Restaurant began with Engracia “Lola Asiang” Cruz Reyes, widely recognized as “The Mother of Filipino Cooking.” Through her profound passion for food, hospitality, and Filipino culinary heritage, she built a restaurant that would eventually become one of the country's most respected and iconic dining destinations.

Founded in 1936, the restaurant quickly became renowned for its flavorful specialties and warm, family-oriented atmosphere. Today, we continue to honor her vision by preserving heritage recipes and maintaining the same quality, consistency, and warmth that guests have loved for decades.
